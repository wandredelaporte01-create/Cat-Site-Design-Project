Cats and Cantaloupe

Cats and cantaloupe have a strange relation. Cats are carnivorous animals, while cantaloupe is a melon.

Cats are known to enjoy cantaloupe, usually exclusively among fruits. This is because the amino acids that the cantaloupe gives off, are processed by the cat to be the amino acids found in meat. Thus, cats believe that it is meat and will try to eat it. In simpler terms, cats like cantaloupe because cantaloupe smells like meat to them, and cats are carnivores.
